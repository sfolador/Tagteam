## Tagteam 2.1.1

### A bar for the Fable weekly limit

Claude plans now cap Fable with its own weekly limit, on top of the 5-hour and 7-day ones. The live account card shows it as a third bar under 7d, with the percentage used and when it resets; hover it for the full label. For the other accounts, it appears in the tooltip of their usage bar. If Anthropic adds a weekly limit for another model, it shows up the same way, labelled with the model's name.
